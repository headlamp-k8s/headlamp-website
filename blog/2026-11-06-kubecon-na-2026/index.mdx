# Headlamp Team at KubeCon NA 2026

[KubeCon + CloudNativeCon North America 2026](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-north-america/) is taking place next week and we are happy to be heading to Salt Lake City again! Here is where you can find us.

<!--truncate-->

## Maintainer Summit

On Sunday, November 8, we will attend the [Maintainer Summit](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-north-america/features-add-ons/maintainer-summit/) to connect with other maintainers before the main event kicks off. This pre-event gathering is honestly one of our favorite parts of KubeCon.

## Project Pavilion

We will be at the Headlamp kiosk (**P-5B**) in the [Project Pavilion](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-north-america/features-add-ons/project-engagement/#project-pavilion) on **Tuesday, November 10:** 3:00 PM - 7:30 PM MST. Whether you are an active contributor, a user, or just curious about Kubernetes UIs, come say hi!

## See You There

Looking forward to another great KubeCon!
